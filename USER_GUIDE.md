# 📝 Sign Language Recognition - Full User Guide

Welcome to your **"Fully Powerful"** Sign Language Translation system. This guide explains every gesture and feature available.

---

## 1. Static Gestures (Letters & Numbers)
These are recognized when you hold a steady pose for **0.3 seconds**.

*   **A-Z (Alphabet):** Perform the standard American Sign Language (ASL) shapes.
*   **0-9 (Digits):** Perform standard digit shapes.
*   **Spacebar:** Hold your **Open Hand** (like a '5') to add a space.

---

## 2. Dynamic Motion (Quick Actions)
These are triggered by **fast movements** using the "Motion-Sense" engine.

*   **Swipe Left** ⬅️: Move your hand horizontally to the left. 
    *   *Action:* Deletes the last character (Backspace).
*   **Swipe Right** ➡️: Move your hand horizontally to the right.
    *   *Action:* Adds a Space.
*   **Zoom Out / Clear** 🔙: Move your hand vertically downward quickly.
    *   *Action:* Clears the entire sentence.

---

## 3. Keyboard Controls (Mode Switching)
Press these keys on your keyboard while the app is running:

*   **`1`**: **LETTERS MODE** — Optimizes the AI to see only the Alphabet (A-Z).
*   **`2`**: **NUMBERS MODE** — Optimizes the AI for digits (0-9).
*   **`3`**: **ALL MODE** — Full recognition of everything.
*   **`Q`**: **Quit** the application.

---

## 4. Conflict Resolution (The Smart Popup)
Sometimes different signs look identical (like 'C' and '5').
*   When a conflict is detected, a **Resolution Overlay** appears.
*   **Press the key** on your keyboard that matches what you meant (e.g., press `5` for 5, `C` for C, or `Space` for Space).

---

## 5. Pro-Tips for Best Results
1.  **Good Lighting:** Make sure your hand is well-lit so the camera doesn't see "shadows."
2.  **Steady Path:** For Swipes, move your hand in a straight line.
3.  **Hold Still:** For letters, try to keep your hand steady for just a fraction of a second.
